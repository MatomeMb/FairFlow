# Lens — .things that Matter

**Short tagline (132 chars max):** Discover and interact with portable commerce objects across the web. Click matter:// links to see product details instantly.

---

## Full Description (Chrome Web Store)

Lens brings Object-Oriented Commerce to your browser. When you encounter a `matter://` link — in social posts, messages, or anywhere on the web — Lens resolves it and shows you a compact product overlay: title, price, image, and a direct path to checkout.

**What Lens does:**
- Intercepts `matter://` links and displays product information in an overlay
- Works with Matter Photos (images with embedded commerce data)
- Caches product data for fast repeat views
- No account required; works immediately after installation

**For merchants and developers:**
Lens is part of the .things that Matter ecosystem. Compile your product catalog into portable commerce objects using our API, then share `matter://` links anywhere. Lens makes those links actionable for your audience.

**Privacy:** Lens fetches product data only when you click a matter:// link. We do not track your browsing. See our Privacy Policy for details.

**Requirements:** Chrome 88 or later.
