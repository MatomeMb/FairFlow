Privacy Policy — Lens Extension

.things that Matter | Last updated: February 2026

Overview

Lens is a browser extension that resolves matter:// links and displays product information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

Product data: When you click a matter:// link, Lens fetches product metadata (title, price, image URL, checkout URL) from the Shadow Index API. This data is temporarily cached in your browser (IndexedDB) for performance. No product data is sent to us.

Analytics: We do not collect analytics, tracking, or personal data through the extension. We do not track your browsing history.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect: browsing history, personal identifiers, location data, or any information about sites you visit unless you explicitly click a matter:// link.

Third-Party Services

Lens may connect to the Shadow Index API (configurable; default: thingsthatmatter.com) to resolve hashes. API requests include only the product hash and standard HTTP headers. No cookies or tracking identifiers are sent.

Permissions

storage: Used to cache product data locally (IndexedDB).
activeTab, scripting: Required to inject the overlay when you click matter:// links.
host_permissions (http/https): Required to resolve matter:// links and fetch product data.

Updates

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here. Continued use of Lens after updates constitutes acceptance.
