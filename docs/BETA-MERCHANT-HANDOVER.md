# Beta Merchant Handover

This guide helps new merchants onboard quickly and know what to expect during beta.

## One-time setup

1. Sign up as a merchant and log in to the Merchant Dashboard.
2. Open `KYC & Payouts` and start verification.
3. Upload required KYC details/documents and submit.
4. Wait for review approval before payouts unlock.
5. Add bank details in `My Account`.
6. Create your first product and publish it.

## Expected timelines

- KYC review target: **under 48 hours**.
- First payout eligibility: once KYC is approved and qualifying sales are complete.
- First payout release: based on current payout cycle and minimum threshold rules.

## Troubleshooting FAQ

### Why is my payout still pending?

Payouts can remain pending if KYC is not approved yet, payout minimum is not reached, or order release conditions are not complete.

### Webhook failed — what now?

If payment is completed but order status is delayed, contact support with your order ID and payment reference so we can reconcile quickly.

### My KYC status says pending for too long

If pending exceeds 48 hours, contact support with your merchant email so we can escalate manual review.

### I updated bank details but do not see payout changes

Bank updates affect future payout runs. Existing queued payout records keep their current processing state.

### I cannot see my product in the public catalog

Ensure product status is published, stock is above zero, and no archive flag is set.
